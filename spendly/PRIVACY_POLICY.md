# Privacy Policy for Spendly

**Last Updated: January 1, 2026**

## Introduction

Spendly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data You Provide
- **Transaction Data**: Income and expense records you manually enter
- **Categories**: Custom categories you create
- **Budget Information**: Budget limits and savings goals you set
- **Preferences**: App settings like currency, language, and theme

### Data Stored Locally
All your financial data is stored **locally on your device** using secure storage. We do not have access to your personal financial information.

## How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to:
- Display your transactions and financial summaries
- Calculate budgets and savings progress
- Generate insights and analytics
- Provide the core functionality of the app

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored locally on your device
- We use AsyncStorage for secure local data persistence
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- Backup files are created locally and shared only when you choose to export

## Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:
- **Expo**: For app development and updates
- **App Store / Google Play**: For app distribution

These services have their own privacy policies.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access all your data within the app
- Export your data at any time
- Delete all your data by uninstalling the app
- Modify or delete individual transactions

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
